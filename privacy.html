Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This policy covers the Tales of the Ages website and the Tales of the Ages publishing application (the "App"), a private tool that uploads videos to the Tales of the Ages YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the owner of the Tales of the Ages YouTube channel, to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Google data the App accesses

The App requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos and set their thumbnails on the authorizing channel. It does not read, collect or store viewer data, comments, subscriber lists, analytics, emails, contacts or any other personal information.

How data is used and stored

  • The OAuth access token issued by Google is stored only on the channel owner's private server and is used only to upload the channel's own videos.
  • Video files, titles, descriptions and tags uploaded by the App are created by the channel owner.
  • No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all uploads; the stored token is then deleted from the server.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
